Overview
As part of Phase 1 of your course project, each team is required to submit a one-page project summary defining the scope, goals, and proposed methodology for your selected application. Your project must be designed and implemented within the MATLAB environment (using toolboxes such as Computer Vision, Image Processing, and Deep Learning Toolboxes).

Please ensure your team as a group completes and submits a single one-page summary containing the following sections:

Required Sections
	•	Project Title and Team Members
	•	Team Members: List the names and student IDs of all team members (5 students per group).
	•	Problem Statement
	•	Briefly describe the real-world issue, challenge, or application domain (e.g., Smart City/Transport, Healthcare/Biometrics, Robotics, or Interactive AI Systems) your project addresses.
	•	Explain why solving this problem using Computer Vision techniques is necessary or beneficial and how it is different from existing solutions.
	•	Project Objectives
	•	Outline 4–5 specific, measurable goals you aim to achieve by the end of the semester.
	•	Example objectives: Image acquisition/sensor integration, specific feature detection/segmentation accuracy, implementation of a custom algorithm from scratch, build a MATLAB App Designer GUI, or real-time live video testing.
	•	Proposed Methodology & Pipeline
	•	Explain your planned technical approach to tackle the problem. Regardless of your specific topic, your pipeline should outline how you plan to handle the following core stages:
	•	Image/Data Acquisition: Describe how you will acquire images or video streams (e.g., live webcam/camera feed directly into MATLAB, or collecting/downloading a baseline image dataset from a public repository).
	•	Project Dataset Status & Readiness: Provide a concise statement specifying how your team acquired or plans to collect/download the baseline dataset (e.g., self-captured via camera or sourced from a public repository), along with its current readiness level (e.g., fully collected, preprocessed, labeled, or undergoing collection).
	•	Preprocessing: Detail the image processing techniques you plan to apply for image enhancement, noise reduction, or contrast adjustment (e.g., Gabor filtering, histogram equalization, morphological operations).
	•	Detection & Segmentation: Explain how you will locate and isolate the Region of Interest (ROI) or object within the frame (e.g., active contours, background subtraction, color/threshold segmentation, or U-Net).
	•	From-Scratch Algorithmic Step: Identify at least one core computer vision algorithm that your team will write from first principles/mathematical equations in MATLAB without relying on high-level library functions (e.g., Harris corner detection, RANSAC alignment, template matching, background subtraction, or custom filtering or others).
	•	Feature Extraction & Learning/Classification: Specify the features you will extract (e.g., SIFT, SURF, HOG, etc.) and/or the machine learning / deep learning model used for recognition/classification (e.g., CNN, template matching, or pretrained MATLAB models).
	•	Output & Visualization: Describe how the final classification, bounding box, trajectory, or decision support visual will be displayed to the end-user (e.g., live overlay on video feed, or MATLAB App Designer interface).

Important Policy Reminder
	•	Environment: All primary coding, algorithm implementation, and UI design must be executed in MATLAB.
	•	Academic Integrity & Generative AI Policy: Generative AI or automated code-generation tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Copilot) are strictly prohibited for writing, debugging, or documenting project code. All code must be original and fully understood by every team member, as individual code modifications and live defenses will take place during final evaluations in Weeks 14–15 in shaAllah.
